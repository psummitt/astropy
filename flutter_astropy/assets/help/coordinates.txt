Astropy Coordinates Help
========================

This module provides classes for representing celestial coordinates and transforming them between different systems.

Common systems:
- ICRS (International Celestial Reference System)
- Galactic
- AltAz (Altitude-Azimuth)
- FK5, FK4

Usage in Flutter:
Enter RA and Dec in the fields provided and click "Convert" to see the equivalent coordinates in the Galactic system.
